Course Syllabus: High School & College Algebra (MATH 101)

Course Overview:
This course provides a comprehensive foundation in algebraic principles, functions, equations, and mathematical problem-solving.

Chapter 1: Linear Equations and Inequalities
Topic 1.1: Solving Linear Equations
Subtopic: One-step and multi-step equations in one variable
Subtopic: Equations with variables on both sides and special cases (identities and contradictions)
Topic 1.2: Graphing Linear Equations
Subtopic: The Cartesian coordinate plane, intercepts, and slope of a line
Subtopic: Slope-intercept form (y = mx + b) and point-slope form
Topic 1.3: Linear Inequalities
Subtopic: Solving single-variable linear inequalities and interval notation
Subtopic: Graphing inequalities on a coordinate plane

Chapter 2: Systems of Linear Equations
Topic 2.1: Solving Systems Algebraically
Subtopic: Substitution method for linear systems
Subtopic: Elimination (addition) method for linear systems
Topic 2.2: Applications of Linear Systems
Subtopic: Break-even analysis and mixture problems

Chapter 3: Polynomials and Factoring
Topic 3.1: Polynomial Operations
Subtopic: Addition, subtraction, and multiplication of polynomials
Topic 3.2: Factoring Techniques
Subtopic: Greatest common factor (GCF) and factoring by grouping
Subtopic: Factoring trinomials of the form ax^2 + bx + c
Subtopic: Special products: Difference of two squares and perfect square trinomials

Chapter 4: Quadratic Equations and Functions
Topic 4.1: Methods of Solving Quadratics
Subtopic: Solving by factoring and the zero product property
Subtopic: The quadratic formula and the discriminant test
Topic 4.2: Graphing Parabolas
Subtopic: Vertex, axis of symmetry, and x-intercepts
